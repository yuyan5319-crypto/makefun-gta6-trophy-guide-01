    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough is enough, and nothing but the Chapter 1 meditation can be missed. Follow the stages in order; the checklist view tracks each stage as you go.

      The 100% checklist itself only appears after the credits, under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion — but progress made during the story already counts. Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 100% Completion Checklist & Guide; GTABase — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Achievements List.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed — and only possibly. Everything else stays available in free roam after the credits.

      Save habits that keep you safe

      • Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave, and no mission replay.
      • Keep one untouched save from just before the final mission.
      • Never use cheats on your trophy save — cheats should switch off both trophies and saving.
      • Do the Chapter 3 race against Yago the moment it appears (hourglass icon): it is time-sensitive and its strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks.
